## Key Insights

- **Geography is destiny in this portfolio.** All 4 FAIL verdicts are Asian-listed companies (Japan, China, Hong Kong). Three of the four fail on the same structural grounds: government-directed capital allocation or government-destroyed pricing power — the two conditions Buffett most explicitly excludes from his investable universe. This is not a coincidence; it reflects that Buffett's framework was built around businesses with pricing power and management accountability to shareholders, both of which are structurally constrained in state-influenced Asian markets.

- **The WATCH bucket is a tale of two problems.** ABBV, CATL, and 6857 are WATCH primarily on moat durability uncertainty (pharma patent cliffs, battery chemistry evolution, ATE cyclicality), while RELIANCE.NS is WATCH primarily on governance and conglomerate complexity. All four are businesses Buffett might admire; none clear every gate at current conditions. 6857 is the most "PASS-quality" of the WATCHes — only price prevents it from advancing.

- **The two PASSes share a structural pattern.** MCHP (US industrial MCUs) and IBE (Spanish regulated utility) both feature switching-cost or regulated-monopoly moats, management teams with 20+ year track records of shareholder-aligned capital allocation, and prices within Buffett's documented comfort zone. Both have direct Berkshire analogs with documented Buffett endorsement (Precision Castparts and MidAmerican Energy respectively). These are the portfolio's highest-conviction holdings.

- **The Dexter Shoe lesson echoes twice.** Both 600276 (Hengrui) and CATL present the same risk Buffett identified in his worst deal: apparent moats that are not stress-tested against the most plausible secular structural threat. For Hengrui it is VBP government procurement policy; for CATL it is solid-state battery technology displacement. Buffett's lesson from Dexter (2007 letter) — "moat durability must be explicitly tested against secular macro threats, not just current competitors" — is directly applicable to both positions.
